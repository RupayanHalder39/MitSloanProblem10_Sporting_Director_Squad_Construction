market-value source panels. Upstream data are not owned by this repository. Raw data, player-level

excluded. Included CSV files contain aggregate research results and governance metadata only.

Market-value data remain third-party content. Market value is used as a proxy and must not be read as
a transfer fee, budget, spending measure, profit, or accounting value.
