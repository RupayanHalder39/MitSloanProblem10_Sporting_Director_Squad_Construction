# Methodology and evidence boundaries

The unit of analysis is a documented Sporting Director × club × season. The context layer covers 44
rows; the behavioural layer covers 16. Two co-incumbent rows representing one Barcelona squad are
retained for club-context description but excluded from director summaries, leaving 14 rows across
eight directors.

The locked behavioural vector contains median age, under-23 share, 30-plus share, and top-five value
concentration. Results are descriptive: raw distributions, director-level summaries, a full
predeclared Spearman grid, and quality and season sensitivities. No weighting or imputation was
applied.

No causal estimand or counterfactual was defined. No clustering, dimensionality reduction,
mixed-effects model, regression, prediction, hypothesis test, p-value, ranking, rating, or suitability
score exists in the study.

Documentation establishes that a person occupied a role during a season. It does not establish
decision authority over signings, sales, loans, retention, or the whole squad.
